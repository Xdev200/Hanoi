Rules of the Challenge

1

Move Only the Top Disk

You can only pick up and move the topmost disk on any peg stack. You cannot slide middle or bottom disks without moving those above them first.

2

Respect Disk Proportions

Never place a larger disk on top of a smaller disk. Keeping the sizes ordered is the core mechanism that forces recursive paths.

3

The Optimal Move Algorithm

For a game with N disks, the perfect completion requires exactly 2N - 1 moves. Challenge yourself to beat your levels in minimum steps!
