2. Three Ways to Play (Game Modes Explained)
Mechanics

We expanded the game beyond the traditional math puzzle by adding new stand rules and color dynamics:

🏛️ Classic Mode
Levels 1–5 · 3 Stands · 3 to 7 Disks

The traditional Tower of Hanoi. Move all disks from Stand 1 to Stand 3, one disk at a time, never putting a larger disk over a smaller disk.

🧩 Strategic Mode
Levels 6–10 · 4 Stands · 4 to 8 Disks

Introduces a 4th Stand (Reve's Puzzle). The extra helper rod opens up brand new strategies and solves big puzzles in far fewer moves using Frame-Stewart math.

🔴🔵 Dual Tower Mode
Levels 11–14 · 3 or 4 Stands · 2 to 4 Pairs

Disks start separated: Red on Stand 1, Blue on Stand 3. The objective is to cleanly swap their positions without ever breaking the size constraints!

📌 The 2 Golden Rules of Hanoi

Rule 1: One Disk at a Time You can only pick up and move the top-most disk from any stand.
Rule 2: Small on Big Only A bigger disk can NEVER be placed on top of a smaller disk.
3. Key Features & Smart Hint System
Features

💡 How the New Smart Hint System Works

In simple puzzle games, hints only follow a rigid script from the beginning. If the player makes a mistake or gets lost, the hints break. Our smart algorithm solves this problem completely:

⭐ The 3-Star Performance Rating

Each level challenges the player to solve the puzzle in the mathematically minimum number of moves (2ⁿ − 1):

⭐⭐⭐

3 Stars — Master Solve (Par)

Achieved by solving the puzzle in the exact theoretical minimum moves. Shows true mastery.

⭐⭐

2 Stars — Near Optimal

Solved with minor errors, within 1.5× the minimum move threshold.

⭐

1 Star — Cleared

Puzzle was solved successfully, but took additional moves or exploration.

6. Future Scope: Level Progression & Locking System
Future Roadmap
💡 Key Feature Request from the Team: "Levels should be locked and only unlock when the user clears the previous level. Then, upon clearing a world/chapter, the user unlocks a brand new Atmosphere (visual & audio theme)!"
Concept Art: Six thematic floating worlds connected via a sequential unlock progression path.

🗺️ How the Progression Engine Will Work

Instead of having all levels open from day one, players start at World 1: Level 1. Each level completed unlocks the next step in the journey:

Core Mechanics of Level Progression

1. Win to Unlock Next Achieving at least 1 Star on Level N instantly unlocks Level N+1. This ensures casual players are never completely blocked from moving forward.
2. Scaling Difficulty Difficulty scales organically—not just by adding more disks, but by introducing mechanical variants like 4-stands, color-swaps, and one-way restricted moves.
3. The Boss "Star Gates" The final level of a world requires a minimum total star count to enter. This encourages players to replay earlier levels to achieve 3-star (perfect) ratings!

8. Continuous Gameplay & Player Retention
Engagement

Standard Hanoi games suffer from low retention because once a player beats the levels, there is nothing new to do. To keep players playing every single day, we propose four high-impact retention systems:

📅 Daily Challenge & Streaks

A new scrambled puzzle generated every midnight using a Global Seed—meaning every player worldwide faces the exact same unique configuration!

  • Streaks: Playing daily builds a streak multiplier.
  • Rewards: A 7-day streak unlocks an exclusive Golden Disk skin, driving immense habit-forming engagement (boosting DAU by 40-60%).
🏃 Sprint Relay Mode

An intense endless survival mode. Players solve quick 3, 4, and 5-disk puzzles back-to-back with a shared countdown timer!

  • Combo Mechanics: Solving a puzzle in the mathematically perfect number of moves freezes the timer for 5 seconds.
  • Survival: You only have 3 lives. Taking too long or making invalid moves costs a life.
👻 Ghost Race (Async Multiplayer)

The ultimate competitive twist. You race head-to-head against another player's previously recorded run.

  • Visual Feedback: A subtle glowing "ghost" avatar physically moves on the HUD, showing exactly where your opponent was at that specific second.
  • Innovation: No existing Hanoi game on Google Play has this feature!
🏆 Weekly Tournaments & Seasons

Every weekend, a designated set of 5 ultra-hard tournament puzzles opens up.

  • Tiered Leaderboards: Players are placed into Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Master divisions based on their total combined solve time.
  • Season Pass: Points earned in tournaments unlock premium cosmetic effects and avatar borders.
