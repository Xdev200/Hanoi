Hanoi is a native Android educational puzzle game that turns Tower of Hanoi into a guided lesson on recursion, binary representation, and algorithmic thinking.

Features:
- 12 progressive levels across 3-peg and 4-peg variants
- Tap-to-move puzzle board with legal move validation
- Binary move counter and post-level insight cards
- Hint and auto-solver controls
- Recursion tree explanation overlay
- Classic Wood and Light visual themes
- Adaptive launcher icon and splash screen
